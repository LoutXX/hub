Skorozvon Hub

An internal tool for forwarding call data and call recordings to Google Sheets and Google Drive.

What the app does

Skorozvon Hub is a small private service that polls the Skorozvon call-center API for successful calls, writes a summary row for each call to a Google Sheet, and uploads the call recording (an MP3 file) to a folder on the owner's Google Drive, so the recording can be found later by phone number or tax ID.

Why it asks for access to Google Drive

The app requests access to Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive) for one purpose only: to upload call recordings into a single folder chosen by the account owner and to set a "anyone with the link can view" permission on the uploaded files. It does not read, modify or delete any other files.

Who uses it

The app is used by its owner only (a single Google account that authorizes it once). It is not offered to the public.
